About the designer

Vidal Coscolin Gomez

For Vidal, creativity begins at home. His father’s craftsmanship and his mother’s work as a seamstress and designer shaped an early appreciation for detail and personal expression.

With family roots in Catalunya, his story brings together craft, clothing and a love of dance. That creative heritage lives on in the world of Coscolin.

Download our app

Add Coscolin to your home screen, directly from our website. No app store download is needed.

For iPhone +

Open the Coscolin website in Safari on your iPhone.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an upward arrow). It may be inside the browser menu.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Look for the C icon on your home screen.

For Android +

Open the Coscolin website in Chrome on your Android phone.

  1. Tap Chrome’s three-dot menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install or Add to confirm.

Already on your phone? Open the C icon. Shopping requires an internet connection.
